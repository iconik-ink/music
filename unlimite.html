Frequently Asked Questions

  • How am I allowed to use the music I license from PremiumBeat?
    You can use the music in videos, podcasts, games, and apps according to your license type. Personal use is covered in all plans, while commercial and client work requires Standard or higher plans.
  • How does the PremiumBeat Unlimited Subscription work?
    Our unlimited subscription gives you access to our entire library of music and sound effects. You can download as much as you need while your subscription is active, with new content added weekly.
  • How do I switch to the Unlimited Subscription from my current subscription or pack?
    You can upgrade or change your subscription at any time from your account dashboard. Any unused portion of your current plan will be prorated and applied to your new subscription.
  • Does the Unlimited Music Subscription cover TV, broadcast, radio, film or in-app use?
    Extended coverage for TV, broadcast, radio, film, and in-app use is available in our Premium, Business, and Enterprise plans. Please refer to the license comparison tables above for detailed coverage information.
